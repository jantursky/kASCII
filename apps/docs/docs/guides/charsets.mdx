## Your own ramp

```kotlin
val mine = Charsets.custom(" .oO@")
```

List the glyphs from lightest to darkest. If you do not know their weight, `Charset.Ramp.byCoverage(name, glyphs) { coverage(it) }` orders them from a measured ink coverage per glyph.

## Braille and quadrants

Both are bit masks. For Braille, each of the eight dots in a cell is lit when its tone sample is above the `threshold` (0.5 by default). Change it with `Charsets.Braille.withThreshold(0.3f)`. Because one cell covers 2 by 4 samples, Braille draws smooth shapes without shrinking the cell, which is why the line chart and gauge components use it.

## Fonts

Braille and katakana are not in most monospace fonts. Kascii bundles JetBrains Mono for Latin, box drawing and blocks, and a subset of GNU Unifont for Braille and katakana. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and their licenses ship with the library.
