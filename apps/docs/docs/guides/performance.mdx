## Where the time goes

- **Field sampling** is `cols * subX * rows * subY` calls of `Field.tone` per frame. Braille samples eight times per cell.
- **Glyph lookup** is cheap.
- **Drawing** is the big cost on the CPU path: one atlas blit per cell.

The GPU path moves drawing to a shader, and the clock reads time only while drawing, so frames do not recompose with the Cpu and Gpu renderers. The Text renderer and the text-effect components recompose on every tick.

## Budget

The target is the display's frame time, 16 ms at 60 Hz, split between sampling and drawing. Measured numbers per piece on a mid-range Android device and in a browser are not available yet: the benchmark runs are still to do, and this page will list them when they exist.

## Tips

- Raise `cell` before lowering the frame rate. Doubling the cell width quarters the work.
- Prefer `Charsets.Braille` over a smaller cell when you want detail: eight dots per cell cost samples, not draws.
- Keep fields cheap. Precompute anything that does not depend on `t`.
- Use `Rng(seed)` outside the per-frame path, or cache stateful pieces by frame index.
- Offscreen pieces pause, but a piece you keep composed offscreen still holds its memory. Remove it from composition when you can.
